Deleting a workspace

Deleting a workspace removes its history for every member of the team, including comments, files, and the audit log.

This cannot be undone

Make sure you have a backup first, because support cannot restore a deleted workspace after thirty days.

Only owners and admins can delete a workspace. Members see the option disabled, and guests cannot open the settings page at all.

If the workspace is connected to a paid plan, cancel the subscription before deleting it, otherwise billing continues until the end of the cycle.

For large workspaces, the deletion job runs in the background and can take several hours to finish, depending on the number of files.
